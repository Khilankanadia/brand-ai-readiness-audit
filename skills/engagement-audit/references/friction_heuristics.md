# On-Site Engagement & Friction Heuristics

When visitors arrive (especially referred by AI assistants or search engines), they have high intent but low patience.

## Core Engagement Evaluation Rules
1. **The 5-Second Orientation Rule**:
   - Within 5 seconds of landing, a user must understand:
     1. Who is this?
     2. What do they do?
     3. What action should I take next?
   - Failure mode: Hero banner has only abstract artwork or vague slogans ("Innovating Tomorrow").
2. **Cognitive Load in Navigation**:
   - Optimal primary navigation: 4–7 items.
   - $> 10$ unorganized links leads to analysis paralysis and immediate tab closure.
3. **Context Retention**:
   - Deep landing pages must have persistent breadcrumbs and clear link back to root.
4. **Trust Signals**:
   - Verifiable contact channels, About page, and privacy policies establish legitimacy.
